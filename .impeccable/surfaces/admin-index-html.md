# Admin analytics

Scope: private operate surface for the owner. Mode: operate.

Audience is Naijei, checking the family of sites. The task is to filter by site and date and read pageviews, visits, estimated uniques, top pages, referrers, and the coverage gap. Constraints: match the public site's type, palette, and light/dark behavior; do not change the public site; label fixtures; leave real unconfigured data empty.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Read collected traffic for the naijei.com family in the portfolio's own chrome, and refuse the generic admin of a sidebar plus three metric cards.
OWN-WORLD: Google Sans, ink on white and night-text on #1F1F1F, hairline rounded surfaces, the soft card shadow, pills, the N mark, and blue/red/yellow/green only to tell sites apart. Dark unless the owner chose light.
STORY: The owner picks a site and a range, sees pageviews, visits, and whether uniques exist, then pages, referrers, and a plain account of what was not measured.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A fixed 4rem bar holds the N mark, Analytics, site tabs with a 3px underline in that site's color, the theme control, and sign out. A yellow band appears only for a fixture or an empty preview. One rounded surface holds the range title, date controls, three figures, and the trend line in the active site color. The line draws once; that is the only authored motion.
FORM: Brief-pinned to match naijei.com. No concept-seed roll; the request specified the visual system and the modules. Seed key: brief-pinned.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

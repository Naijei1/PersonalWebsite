# Gnarly landing

Scope: marketing page for the Gnarly indoor navigation project, served at /gnarly-land/. Mode: persuade.

Audience is someone deciding whether to open the live map, watch the existing film, or read the code. They may be in a campus building or at a laptop. Success is understanding the scan-publish-navigate loop and taking one of those three actions. Proof is the real companion, the YouTube film, and the repository. Do not invent counts, quotes, awards, or captures.
